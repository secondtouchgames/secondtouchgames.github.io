Volt Surge Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026.

Volt Surge (com.samh.voltoverload) is provided by Formline LLC, a Utah limited liability company, which publishes games under the Second Touch name. Business address: 7533 S Center View Ct Ste R, West Jordan, UT 84084, USA. Privacy and support contact: secondtouch.hq@gmail.com.

Gameplay and local storage

No account or login is required. Scores, progress and settings are stored on your device. Gameplay does not ask for your name, email, contacts, payment details or precise GPS location. We do not operate an account database for this game. This does not mean the app collects no data: advertising services process information as described below.

Advertising and consent

The production app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show advertising and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and record applicable consent choices. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalized, non-personalized or limited. Non-personalized ads can still involve data processing.

Google's advertising SDK collects and shares device or other identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID; IP address, from which approximate location may be inferred; app and ad interactions such as launches, taps and video views; and diagnostic/performance information. Diagnostic data may include crash information where supported by the final SDK. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention/security. Data sent by Google's Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

UMP processes consent and privacy-choice information to decide whether ads may be requested. Where required, the app provides a Privacy choices entry point to revisit consent. Withdrawing consent does not itself erase data already held by Google. This policy covers Google AdMob and UMP, not additional advertising mediation networks.

Your choices and deletion

You can clear local scores and progress using Android's app storage controls or by uninstalling the app. Android settings let you reset or delete the advertising ID and manage available advertising privacy controls. Where applicable you can change consent through the app's Privacy choices option. For information Google holds, use My Ad Center and Google's privacy controls. We cannot promise to delete data independently held by Google. Email us with privacy questions; we will explain what we can control and direct requests to the appropriate provider.

Retention and providers

Local gameplay data remains until you clear it or remove the app, subject to your device's backup settings. Google determines retention of data it processes under its own policies. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Children

Volt Surge is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its providers change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
